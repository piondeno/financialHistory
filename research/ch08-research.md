## §7 深度分析 事實核對

- Strong 教義：1922「unofficially abandoned the gold standard」、大膽 OMO、開啟聯準會買賣國債；Keynes《A Tract on Monetary Reform》（1923）以其為例【Federal Reserve Bank of New York】

- Kindleberger：Strong 若未在 1928 死（大蕭條前一年）「might have been able to maintain stability in the international financial system」；Rothbard 反論：「it was Strong's manipulations that caused the Depression in the first place」【Benjamin Strong Jr.】
- NY Fed：資產最大、交易最活躍、最具影響力；1914-11-16 開業；Strong 死前「de facto leader of the entire Federal Reserve System」【Federal Reserve Bank of New York】
